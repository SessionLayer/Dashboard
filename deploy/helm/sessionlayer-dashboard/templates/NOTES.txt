Point your ingress controller at that Service and terminate TLS there. The
image sends HSTS on every response, and the bundle's OIDC flow refuses a
cleartext endpoint, so plain http reaches a login that cannot complete.

The endpoints above are read by the container at start, not baked into the
image, and the Content-Security-Policy is derived from them in the same pass -
so `helm upgrade --set endpoints.cpBaseUrl=...` repoints this console without a
rebuild. A value the container will not accept stops it starting, with the
reason on stdout:
